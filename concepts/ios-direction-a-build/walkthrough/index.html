Sleevy for iOS · Direction A

Before and after

Direction A moves every screen to one quiet, monochrome style. A Saved Item is one line. The shader art is gone. On the iPhone Duo, the Reader View becomes a book. Each section shows a screen on master on the left and the new build on the right, with notes on what changed.

Before: master e1f94af5 After: feat/ios-direction-a, 4 commits All data: Demo Mode Unit tests: 142 passed Click a screenshot to make it larger
01

One style, no colour

Black, white and greys. The only colour comes from favicons, the toggle and Delete.

02

One line per Saved Item

A favicon and a title. The domain and age leave the row. Touch and hold shows the Preview Summary.

03

Large titles with a count

Root screens put the count under the large title. Pushed screens show it as the first row.

04

No shader art

The glow heroes, the folder cards, their playground and the folder colour picker are removed.

The Reader View starts with the title and the Preview Summary. On the Duo it is set as pages.

01

Inbox

The Inbox is the first screen and the one you see most. It now reads like a list of titles, not a feed.

Before

  • A coloured glow behind the title.
  • Two lines per row: the title, then the domain. The age is on the right.
  • One long list, with no groups.

After

  • One line per Saved Item: the favicon and the title. More saves fit on one screen.
  • "19 unread" is now the subtitle of the large title.
  • Groups by day: Today, Yesterday, Earlier.
  • No glow. The black goes to the top edge.
  • The Library tab has a new icon: a row of books.
02

Touch and hold a row

The row lost its domain and age, so touch and hold gives them back, with the Preview Summary. This is where you read about a save before you open it.

Before

  • The row lifts, with four actions: Mark Read, Copy Link, Share, Delete.
  • No preview, and no way to file the save.

After

  • Preview Summary card: site and age, title, summary and Tags.
  • Move to Folder is a submenu, so you can file a save from any list.
  • The same menu shows in the Inbox, Library, Folder View, Tag, Search and Profile.
03

Library

The biggest visual change. The shader cards are replaced by a sidebar of Folders and Tags.

Before

  • One large shader card per Folder, each in its own colour.
  • Under the cards, a list of all saves.
  • Tags show only inside Filters.

After

  • Folders and Tags are two sections that fold, with a count on each row.
  • A globe marks a Published Folder. Touch and hold a Folder to edit it, publish it or delete it.
  • New Folder is the last row of the Folders section.
  • "29 saves" is the subtitle. The sort button holds the sort order and Filters.
04

Folder View

A Folder now looks like every other list. Its colour hero is gone.

Before

  • A glow hero behind a small inline title.
  • Two-line rows with the domain and the age.

After

  • Large title, then a first row: "4 saves · 2 unread · On your profile".
  • One-line rows. The Unread Dot is on the right, and read saves are grey.
  • "On your profile" tells you that this is a Published Folder.
05

Tag New

A Tag in the Library opens a list of every save that carries it, across all Folders.

What it does

  • Same layout as a Folder View: large title, a count row, one-line rows.
  • "7 saves · 5 unread" for the Tag Design.
  • The sort button and the touch-and-hold menu work as in the Library.
06

Reader View

On the iPhone the Reader View still scrolls. The top of the article and the toolbar changed.

Before

  • The title is twice on screen: small in the bar, large in the page.
  • The summary is a plain first paragraph.
  • One menu (⋯) for all actions.

After

  • Up and down step through the list that you opened the save from.
  • Mark Read and Move to Folder are buttons in the bar.
  • The site shows with its favicon, and the Preview Summary is a card that folds.
  • Prose is set at a smaller reading size. Text Size stays the same from article to article.
07

Search

Search keeps its layout. Its results now use the shared row.

What changed

  • Results are one-line rows with the same touch-and-hold menu as the Inbox.
  • Search used its own copy of the row and swipe actions. That copy is deleted.
  • The background is the system black, the same as on every other screen.
08

Profile

Your public page in the app. It keeps its content and drops the glow.

Before

  • A blue glow behind the avatar.
  • Month headers in capitals, and two-line rows with ages.

After

  • No glow. The avatar, name and Make Private stay the same.
  • Month headers in normal case: "October 2026".
  • One-line rows, as in every other list.
09

Settings

Settings already used the system style, so it shows almost no change.

What changed

  • The Folder Card Playground (Debug builds only) is gone, with the folder cards.
  • Theme, Reader View, Account and Source Name are the same.
10

Sign in

The first screen a new user sees. It now uses the same black as the app behind it.

Before

  • An animated colour field with blurred shapes.
  • The mark and the buttons sit in the middle.

After

  • Black, with the white mark, the name Sleevy and "Save it now. Read it later."
  • The buttons move down, where your thumb is.
11

Landscape iPhone

The same screens turn with the phone. One fix came from these screenshots: the Preview Summary card was cut off in landscape.

Upside-down is not a supported orientation on the iPhone, so it shows the portrait layout. All four orientations are on contact sheets 01–04.
12

iPhone Duo

On the Duo, the app uses a vertical rail in place of the tab bar. Direction A uses that rail to set the Reader View as a book: one page on the cover display, and a two-page spread on the inner display, split on the hinge.

Cover display Closed, portrait

Before

  • The Reader View scrolls, next to the rail.
  • The rail holds Back, ⋯ and the haptic scroll wheel.

After

  • The article is one page, with the page number at the foot.
  • The rail adds the Article Outline and Text Size.
  • The haptic scroll wheel stays. It now turns to a section's page, and its deck shows the page numbers.
Inner display Book or Open, portrait (the wide way)

Before

  • A split view: a list of saves on the left, one wide column of prose on the right.
  • The lines run across most of the display, which is hard to read.

After

  • A two-page spread, split on the hinge, set with TextKit 1. Pages 1 and 2 are side by side.
  • Short lines, ragged right, with hyphens. Nothing crosses the hinge.
Article Outline the left page becomes the contents page
The Article Outline shows the Preview Summary, then each section with its page number. Tap a section to go to its page.
Lists on the Duo
Other postures
Book and Open look the same, pixel for pixel. The layout follows the display and the orientation, not the hinge angle. Contact sheets 05–16 show every posture in all four orientations.
